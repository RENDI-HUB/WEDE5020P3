Organization overview Name: gift to the nation History Gift to the Nation began as a small community organization dedicated to uplifting those in immediate need. Through the generous support and donations of South African citizens, it has since grown into a nationwide non-profit organization, expanding its reach to help all south africans who aren’t in a position to help themselves Mission and vision The mission of Gift to the Nation is to tackle poverty and restore dignity as we believe that everyone is entitled to this regardless of their financial background, this will help create a society where every individual has the chance to thrive. Target audience Our target audience includes underprivileged families, orphaned and vulnerable children, and individuals facing severe economic hardship, ensuring that our support reaches the most marginalized and deserving members of society. As well as possible sponsors and volunteers who are the backbone of the organization Strengths The Gift to the Nation website’s strengths lie in its clear, mission-focused presentation that immediately communicates the organization’s purpose and values to visitors and makes user interaction with potential volunteers & donors easy. Its main weakness is that the donation process could be streamlined for faster transactions, and there could be more interactive elements to engage users. For areas of improvement, we propose enhancing the mobile responsiveness, integrating social media feeds for live updates, and adding a dedicated blog section for regular news and stories. Regarding design and user experience, the interface should remain clean and intuitive, with a strong visual hierarchy and easily accessible navigation to ensure a pleasant and efficient journey for every user. 4.

Goals for website The goals for the website are to showcase impact stories to demonstrate the difference donations make, providing a platform for volunteers to donate to the cause and make a difference in people’s lives at the click of a button.
